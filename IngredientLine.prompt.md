Ingredients are one balanced run of Title Case names separated by commas — never a bulleted list, never a table.

```jsx
<IngredientLine items={['Hibiscus', 'Blue Lotus', 'Rose Petals', 'Orange Peel']} />
```

Keep the label's own order and spelling (Cats Claw, not Cat's Claw; Licorice Root, not Liquorice). It wraps on a 34ch measure with balanced lines.
