02 / three cuts

What is costing you

Cut one

"So" is doing nothing

Cut two

Right now it is "same as what I just said." That is dead airtime and a panel notices. Use a second example instead. Handling a fully booked day, or relaying bad news to a patient.

Cut three

"Calm" lands four times in a row

Pulling him aside, staying calm, calming him down, being calm yourself. Say it once, then show it. The refund on the spot is what actually proves it.
