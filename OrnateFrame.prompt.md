Wraps a surface in the brand's double hairline border with leaf ornaments at the corners — the strongest single signal that something is Britanical Blends packaging.

```jsx
<OrnateFrame color="color-mix(in srgb, var(--wine-700) 45%, transparent)" inset={12}>
  <LabelContent />
</OrnateFrame>
```

Tint the frame with the product accent, not neutral grey. Drop corners on surfaces under ~200px wide, where the ornaments collapse into specks.
