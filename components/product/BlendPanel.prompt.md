Composes SectionLabel, BenefitList and BrewGuide into the panel printed on the product card. Use it rather than rebuilding the two columns.

```jsx
<BlendPanel benefits={['Supports immunity', 'Aids digestion', 'Anti-inflammatory', 'Energising & grounding']} />
```

Tint borderColor with the accent on coloured pages. The panel is the only rounded rectangle on the card — everything else is square or framed.
