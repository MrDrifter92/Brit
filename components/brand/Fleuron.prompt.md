The house divider — a hairline rule interrupted by a floral typographic ornament. It appears once per label, between the wordmark and the product name.

```jsx
<Fleuron width={220} glyphColor="var(--bark-600)" />
```

Use the Unicode ornament rather than an SVG. Alternatives seen in the artwork: ❦ (default), ❧, ✦. One fleuron per surface — a second reads as decoration for its own sake.
