The three-word brand strapline. It sits directly under the wordmark on banners, and inside the coloured footer band on product cards.

```jsx
<Strapline font="sans" color="var(--paper-100)" size={15} />
```

Use font="serif" on printed/packaging surfaces and font="sans" (Jost Light) over photography, matching the supplied lifestyle banners. Keep the words as-is; the separator is a middot, never a bullet or a slash.
