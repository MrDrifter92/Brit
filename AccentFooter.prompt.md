Closes every product card, edge to edge, with no radius and no border. Its colour is how a customer tells the three blends apart at a glance.

```jsx
<div data-accent="wildwood">
  <AccentFooter quote={['Rooted in nature.', 'Made for you.']} />
</div>
```

Wheat band takes ink-coloured type; wine and moss bands take cream. It always spans the full card width — inset it and the card stops looking like packaging.
