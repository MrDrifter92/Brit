The circular brand roundel — use it as the site avatar, jar-lid sticker, or a stamp in the corner of a layout. It is a supplied raster asset, not drawn in code.

```jsx
<Logomark size={120} src="../../assets/logo-roundel.png" />
```

Always pass a src that resolves from the page you are building. Minimum legible size is 64px — below that use `<Wordmark size="sm" />` instead. Never place the roundel on a busy photograph without a cream plate behind it.
