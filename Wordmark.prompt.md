Renders the Britanical Blends name in letterspaced serif capitals — use it wherever the brand signs a surface (label header, card header, banner lockup, email footer).

```jsx
<Wordmark size="lg" color="var(--wine-700)" />
```

Sizes: `sm` 12px (jar label), `md` 16px (product card header), `lg` 24px, `xl` 34px, `display` 48px (banner lockup). The trailing letterspace is optically compensated with a matching text-indent, so centred text stays centred. Never bold it and never tighten the tracking below `--tracking-wordmark`.
